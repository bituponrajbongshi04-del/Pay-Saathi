REFUNDS & REVERSALS

Refunds, explained clearly.

Find out how to raise a transaction issue and what information helps the review process.

Information centre Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions Refund Policy Contact Support

1. Purpose and scope

This Refund, Reversal and Cancellation Policy explains how PaySaathi reviews requests relating to account top-ups/add-money requests and services initiated from an active customer dashboard. The outcome depends on the actual payment channel, transaction status, service provider rules and applicable law.

2. Add-money / account-load requests

  • Payment successful and balance credited: the amount is recorded in the customer account. A reversal or refund request will be assessed under the applicable payment arrangement and law; it is not automatically available merely because the customer changes their mind.
  • Payment debited but balance not credited: raise a ticket with the payment reference, date and amount. PaySaathi will check the platform record and, where needed, seek confirmation from the payment gateway/bank. A confirmed failed or reversed payment will be handled through the applicable payment channel.
  • Duplicate load: report both references. The team will verify whether more than one payment was actually captured and whether both amounts were credited.
  • Payment pending: wait for the final status and do not repeat the same payment request until it has been checked. Pending alone does not establish that a refund is due.

3. Recharge, bill payment and other services

  • Failed service: if the account was charged but the provider confirms that the service failed, the transaction will be reviewed for reversal or refund under the provider/payment arrangement.
  • Pending service: the provider may still be processing the request. The final provider status must be checked before a reversal is decided.
  • Successful service: once a recharge, bill payment or other service is successfully delivered, cancellation or refund may not be possible. Any exception is subject to provider rules and applicable law.
  • Incorrect customer details: a request sent using an incorrect mobile number, account number, operator or biller may not be reversible after provider processing. Contact support immediately; recovery is not guaranteed.

4. How to submit a refund or reversal request

Sign in and use the Contact Support page to create a ticket. Provide your registered account details, transaction ID/reference, service type, date, amount, payment reference and a concise description. Attach proof only through a secure upload facility if one is provided. Never share a password, OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV or full card details.

5. Verification and processing time

PaySaathi may check the dashboard ledger, payment gateway/bank confirmation and service-provider response. Processing time depends on the responsible payment channel or provider. We will not promise a fixed number of days unless the applicable provider or law specifies and confirms that timeline. If a refund is approved, it will be routed to the original payment method or adjusted to the account balance only where the applicable arrangement permits it and the customer is informed.

6. Unauthorised or suspicious transactions

Report any suspected unauthorised transaction immediately through support and contact your bank/payment provider without delay. We may temporarily restrict the account while the issue is investigated, where permitted. Reporting an issue does not by itself guarantee a refund; the evidence, payment status and applicable rules will be reviewed.

7. No double benefit

If a service is later confirmed successful after a provisional reversal or adjustment, the transaction may be reconciled and any duplicate credit corrected, subject to applicable law and fair notice to the customer.

8. Complaints and escalation

If you are dissatisfied with the handling of a request, reply to the existing support ticket and quote its ticket ID. Use the published grievance contact or escalation details once they are configured on the Contact Support page. Mandatory rights and timelines under applicable law or the responsible regulated provider's rules remain unaffected.

9. Policy changes

This Policy may be updated when services, payment partners or applicable requirements change. The version displayed on this page applies subject to mandatory legal rights.
